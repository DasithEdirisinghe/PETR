# StreamPETR original 4 m TP localization

Source: `/Volumes/n12893251/dasith_ws/dev_ws/StreamPETR/experiments/center_error_by_range_outputs/matched_centers.csv`. Only the original 4 m matches were used. Rows group matches by GT ego range; columns are the disjoint `[0,0.5)`, `[0.5,1)`, `[1,2)`, `[2,4)` m center-error bands. Cell percentages divide the original 4 m TP count within each class/range row; they are not AP. The cumulative retention figure is also provided.

Original 4 m TPs: 117,456.
