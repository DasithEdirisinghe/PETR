# PETR baseline: disjoint center-error bands

![Disjoint error-band heatmap](fixed_4m_tp_error_bands.svg)

Each class/range row partitions its original 4 m true positives into `[0,0.5)`, `[0.5,1)`, `[1,2)`, and `[2,4)` m. Color is the percentage of 4 m TPs in one band; cell text gives the exact count. The four percentages sum to 100% within every populated row. These are not AP values.
