# PETR baseline: retention of fixed 4 m true positives

![Fixed 4 m TP retention by class, GT range, and threshold](fixed_4m_tp_retention.svg)

Every row starts with the **same predictions originally matched to GT within 4 m**. The percentages say what fraction of those fixed matches also have center error below 0.5, 1, or 2 m. No prediction–GT assignments are changed. Each cell gives `TP count (percentage of 4 m TPs)`. These values are **not AP** or recall over all GT.
